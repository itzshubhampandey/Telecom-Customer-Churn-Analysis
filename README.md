# 📊 Telecom Customer Churn Analysis & Retention Strategy

## 🧾 Overview
This project analyzes customer churn in the telecom industry using Python. The goal is to understand customer behavior, identify churn patterns, and provide actionable insights to improve customer retention.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Identify key factors affecting customer churn  
- Analyze service usage and its impact on retention  
- Compare churn across different customer segments  
- Provide business recommendations  

---

## 📂 Dataset
The dataset includes:
- Customer demographics (gender, senior citizen, etc.)
- Services used (phone, internet, security, support, etc.)
- Churn status (Yes/No)

---

## 🔍 Analysis Performed
- Data Cleaning & Preprocessing  
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)  
- Countplots and distribution analysis  
- Percentage & stacked bar charts  
- Churn comparison across services  

---

## 📈 Key Insights
- Fiber optic users have higher churn  
- Customers without add-on services churn more  
- More services → lower churn  
- No internet users show very low churn  

---

## 💡 Recommendations
- Improve fiber optic service quality  
- Promote add-on services (security, tech support)  
- Offer bundled plans  
- Target high-risk customers  

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Python  
- Pandas  
- Matplotlib  
- Seaborn  

---

## 🎯 Conclusion
Customer engagement through additional services significantly reduces churn. Improving high-risk services and promoting value-added features can help telecom companies retain customers effectively.
